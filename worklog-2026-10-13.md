### 4. Ask-Why (`ask_why.py`)
- Gemini grounded on the Vertex AI Search data store **plus** verified Decision Records passed in the prompt.
- Two kinds of evidence:
  - **Documents** (policy, tickets) from search grounding metadata
  - **Expert knowledge** (Decision Records from interviews)
- Sources are taken from grounding metadata (provable), not from the model's text.
- Retry with exponential backoff on `429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` (5s, 10s, 20s, 40s).
- System instruction rules: cite ticket and record IDs, never guess, say "No recorded rationale found." when nothing explains *why*, under 200 words.
- Guardrails: `max_output_tokens=1024`, plus detection of repetitive output (shows a safe fallback instead of a broken answer).

## Lessons for interviews and the pitch
- **Provenance:** sources come from grounding metadata, which the model cannot invent.
- **Two kinds of knowledge:** written (documents) and captured from people (Decision Records). This is the difference from Glean, Rovo or Copilot.
- **Design for the failure case:** instructions reduce bad output, guardrails catch what slips through, tests prove both.
- **Retrieval at small scale:** with 14 documents, search adds little accuracy; the retrieval layer sits behind one function, so it scales to thousands of tickets without code changes elsewhere.
